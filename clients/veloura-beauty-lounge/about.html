Our Story

Beauty, slowed down.

Veloura started with one simple wish — a place that actually felt as good as it looked. A salon where the music isn't loud, the chairs are comfy, and the work is genuinely thoughtful.

Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Mia.

I've been a nail artist for over a decade and somewhere along the way I realised most salons get the technical part right but completely miss the feeling part. I wanted to fix that — properly.

Veloura is built around small things: ten extra minutes per appointment so nothing feels rushed, tools that are properly sanitised in front of you, polishes I'd happily wear myself, and a team who genuinely loves what they do.

Whether you're here for a quick refresh before a date, a full bridal trial, or just an excuse to sit down for an hour — you're in good hands.
