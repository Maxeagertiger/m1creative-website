Our Story

Born from obsession,
built by vision

In 2018, five filmmakers met in a Johannesburg basement, united by a single conviction: that the most powerful stories are told by those who live them. From that basement to festivals across four continents, Prisma has grown into a 200-member collective spanning 34 countries — each member a world-class visual storyteller in their own right.

We don't hire. We recognise. Every member is selected because their perspective is irreplaceable. Together, we've produced award-winning documentary series, brand campaigns for Fortune 500 companies, and independent art films that have changed conversations.
