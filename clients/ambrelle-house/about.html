Our Story

Home / About
Est. 2009

A Vision Born from Passion

Ambrelle House began as a dream shared by two culinary enthusiasts who believed that dining should be an experience that touches the soul. In a small space in the heart of the city, we opened our doors with a simple mission: to create moments of joy through exceptional food and warm hospitality.

What started as a modest 30-seat restaurant has grown into a beloved destination, but our core values remain unchanged. Every dish that leaves our kitchen carries the same love and attention to detail that defined our first service over fifteen years ago.

Today, Ambrelle House stands as a testament to the power of perseverance, creativity, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. We're not just serving meals—we're crafting memories that last a lifetime.

Our Philosophy

Farm to Table,
Heart to Plate

We believe that extraordinary cuisine begins with extraordinary ingredients. That's why we've built lasting relationships with local farmers, fishermen, and artisan producers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.

Our menu changes with the seasons, celebrating the best that nature has to offer at any given moment. When you dine with us, you're not just enjoying a meal—you're tasting the story of the land, the sea, and the hands that nurtured every ingredient.
