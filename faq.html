GOT QUESTIONS?

frequently asked

Everything you need to know about our web design process, hosting, retainers, and domain management.

Do I need to buy my own domain name and hosting?

+

No, we handle everything. Your secure domain name (.co.za) and cloud hosting are fully included in your monthly retainer.

What happens if I want to cancel my monthly package?

+

Our packages run on a flexible month-to-month basis. There are no lock-in contracts; you can cancel your retainer at any time with 30 days notice.

Can I upgrade my package later on?

+

Absolutely. As your business grows, you can easily scale up from Bronze to Silver or Gold at any stage without losing your existing site data.

How fast will my website be launched?

+

Our turnaround time is 5 to 10 business days depending on your package tier (with 5-day express priority track on Gold) once content and brand details are submitted. We engineer with maximum speed and accuracy.

Are your websites mobile responsive and SEO optimized?

+

Yes! Every single site we engineer is built mobile-first and includes essential SEO metadata, speed optimization, and Google Maps readiness.

How does the 50/50 payment split work?

+

To eliminate risk for you, our projects use a transparent 50/50 payment structure:

1. First 50% Deposit: Paid via Yoco upon project kickoff. This confirms your booking and our team starts building immediately.
2. Final 50% Balance: Billed via PayFast only after your website is 100% complete, reviewed, and approved by you.
3. Monthly Retainer: Managed automatically via PayFast on a 30-day deferred cycle starting 30 days after launch. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

Which payment gateways do you use, and is my payment secure?

+

We use South Africa's most trusted, bank-grade payment providers: Yoco and PayFast (Network International).

Both gateways are PCI-DSS Level 1 certified (the highest global standard) with 256-bit SSL encryption and 3D Secure OTP verification. We accept Visa, Mastercard, and Instant EFT across all major South African banks (FNB, Standard Bank, Capitec, ABSA, Nedbank). Your sensitive card or banking credentials never touch our servers.
